Privacy Policy

AiBhive · Last updated: June 16, 2026

AiBhive (“the app”) is operated by AiBhive (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

What we collect

  • Account info — If you sign in with Google, we receive your Firebase user ID and email address to sync your profile and Hive credits.
  • App content you create — Job listings, notes, chat history stored on your device and optionally synced to our cloud when you are signed in.
  • Hive Magic build requests — Feature descriptions you submit for estimates and builds are processed on our servers.
  • Payment info — Purchases of Hive credits are handled by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Diagnostics — Basic crash and usage logs to keep the service reliable.

Third-party AI and APIs

When you enable Hive Magic or connect your own API keys in Settings, requests may be sent to providers you choose (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic) or to our orchestration service. Do not submit passwords, government IDs, or other highly sensitive data unless you intend for those providers to process it.

How we use data

  • Provide app features (job tracking, AI chat, Hive Magic builds)
  • Authenticate you and sync data across devices
  • Bill Hive credits and prevent abuse
  • Improve stability and security

Storage and retention

Data is stored on your device and, when signed in, in Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure. You may delete local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Contact us to request deletion of cloud data tied to your account.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet — API calls and updates
  • Camera / photos — Attach job listing screenshots (only when you choose)
  • Notifications — Hive Magic build completion alerts

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Use the app without Google sign-in (limited sync) when auth is optional
  • Remove API keys in Settings at any time
  • Sign out to stop cloud sync for your account

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@aibhive.com · aibhive.com/about
