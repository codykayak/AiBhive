# Tartarian / Old World Reset — Documented Finds Directory

**Purpose for AiBhive assistants:** When users ask general Tartarian / Old World / mud-flood / star-fort questions, do **not** invent archives. Point them to these **documented starting leads** (primary-source collections + specific search recipes). Frame answers as *investigation starting points* with citable URLs. Prefer quality over volume. Encourage Fable Scrape → OCR → Communal Library publish so the hive compounds.

**Product hooks:** Research Lab workspace (`/research-lab/workspace`), Communal Library topic `tartarian`, domain pack `chronicling-america-tartaria`, Fable Scrape AI Harvest (text-corpus mode for newspapers).

---

## How to answer general questions

1. Pick **2–4** leads from this directory that match the user’s angle (architecture, newspapers, maps, orphan trains, mud flood).
2. Give **exact start URLs or search recipes** they can paste into Fable Scrape.
3. Suggest settings: Crawl on, max pages 8–12, findings 2–4, Max stealth if blocked; keep runs under a few Hive credits.
4. Remind them to publish cleaned public-domain quotes to Communal Library · Tartarian.
5. Never claim contested historical conclusions as settled fact — cite sources and flag what still needs verification.

## A. Architecture & World’s Fairs (photo / plate archives)

| Lead | Why it matters | Start here |
|------|----------------|------------|
| World’s Columbian Exposition (Chicago 1893) | Massive temporary “White City”; plates, maps, and rebuild narratives | Internet Archive search: `Columbian Exposition 1893 photographs`; David Rumsey maps of fairgrounds |
| Louisiana Purchase Exposition (St. Louis 1904) | Grand fair architecture in the Midwest; under-discussed vs Chicago | `site:archive.org "Louisiana Purchase Exposition" photographs` |
| Panama–Pacific International Exposition (SF 1915) | West Coast fair + earthquake/rebuild context | LOC / Archive.org: `Panama-Pacific International Exposition` |
| Centennial Exhibition (Philadelphia 1876) | Early U.S. fair mega-structures | Archive.org: `Centennial Exhibition 1876` |
| County courthouses 1870–1910 | Repeated “grand stone” builds in small Midwestern/Southern counties | State historical society photo collections + local Sanborn maps |
| “Phoenix” rebuilds after fires | Rapid reconstruction claims — compare newspapers vs photo dates | Pair fire-year newspapers (Chron Am) with city photo books on Archive.org |

**Fable tip:** Prefer collection/search URLs that list **plates or JPGs**, not fair homepages.

### Under-discussed newspaper angles

1. **19th-c. U.S. papers using “Tartar/Tartary”** in non-Asian contexts (trade, people, place names) — harvest quotes with dates/cities.
2. **Small-city “new courthouse / new opera house” boom** 1885–1910 — compare claimed build speed vs labor reports.
3. **Post-fire “rose from ashes in N months”** language — Chicago, Baltimore, San Francisco, smaller towns.
4. **Orphan train arrival notices** in Midwest papers near sudden institutional builds.

**Harvest settings:** Crawl ✓ · max pages 8–12 · link depth 1 · findings 2–4 · text-corpus mode OK · English translate off to save credits.

## D. Orphan trains, foundlings & institutions

Documented research starting points (primary sources, not conclusions):

- National Orphan Train Complex / state historical societies — arrival lists, placement ads.
- Chronicling America: `"orphan train"` + state name (Missouri, Kansas, Iowa, Indiana, Texas).
- City directories: orphan asylum / foundling hospital annual reports on Archive.org.
- Angle: correlate **arrival waves** with **new grand institutional buildings** in the same county/decade — treat as hypothesis to test with dates.

## F. High-signal American city dossiers (starter kits)

Use these as **bundles**: 1 newspaper search + 1 photo/map collection.

1. **Chicago** — 1871 fire rebuild + 1893 Columbian Exposition plates + Chron Am `Chicago` + `exposition` / `rebuilt`.
2. **St. Louis** — 1904 fair + riverfront grade changes + local papers.
3. **San Francisco** — 1906 quake/fire + 1915 PPIE; compare pre/post street grades.
4. **Baltimore** — 1904 fire rebuild coverage in Chron Am.
5. **Smaller Midwest county seats** — courthouse photo books + Chron Am county paper (often under-discussed).
6. **Southern cotton-era cities** — opera houses / exchanges 1880–1910; local papers + Sanborn.
7. **Buffalo / Niagara** — Pan-American Exposition 1901 plates + local Chron Am.
8. **Portland / Seattle** — early 20th-c. “instant city” growth photo books + grade/regrade stories.
9. **New Orleans** — exposition (1884–85) + levee/grade narratives + Chron Am Louisiana papers.
10. **Denver / mining-boom towns** — opera houses, capitol builds, sudden stone grandeur in thin decades.

### Extra under-explored U.S. angles

- **State capitol construction timelines** 1870–1915 — compare cornerstone dates vs “completed in N years” press.
- **Armory / drill hall boom** — large masonry halls in small cities; Chron Am `armory dedicated`.
- **Railroad hotel / depot palaces** — Midwestern junction towns; Archive.org souvenir albums.
- **Asylum / poorhouse campuses** — institutional megastructures; pair with orphan-train counties.
- **Exhibition halls reused as civic buildings** after fairs — track “former exposition” language in papers.
