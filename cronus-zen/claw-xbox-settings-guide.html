Claw Xbox Premium — Settings Guide

Cronus Zen GPC Script • Xbox Series X|S / Xbox One • Call of Duty (BO6 / BO7 / Warzone)

Script file: claw-xbox-premium.gpc
Claw layout: RB = Fire • LB = ADS • D-Pad Right = Grenade • Broken RT bypassed

1. Before You Start

Required in-game settings (Call of Duty)

  • Button Layout: Tactical (or Tactical Flip)
  • Fire: RT (the script converts your RB press to RT)
  • ADS: LT (the script converts your LB press to LT)
  • Lethal / Grenade: D-Pad Right (press D-Pad Right directly)

Loading the script (first time only)

  1. Connect Cronus Zen to your PC using the PROG port (right side).
  2. Open Zen Studio on Windows.
  3. Go to File → Open and select claw-xbox-premium.gpc.
  4. Press F7 to compile. Fix any errors shown in the output panel.
  5. Drag the compiled script to a memory slot (1–8) in the Programmer panel.
  6. Click Program Device to save it to the Zen.
  7. Unplug from PC, connect Zen to Xbox, and select that slot on the Zen OLED.
Note: You only need a PC to load or update the script. All settings below can be changed on your Xbox controller once the script is programmed.

2. Opening the Mod Menu

You can open the menu any time the Cronus is plugged in — at the Xbox home screen or in a match.

ActionController Input
Open Mod MenuHold LT (left trigger) + press VIEW (Menu button, two lines)
ConfirmationController rumbles + mod list appears on Zen OLED screen
Kill Switch (disable all mods)Hold LT + press SHARE

When the Zen first loads, the OLED briefly shows CLAW XBOX and LT+VIEW as a reminder.

3. Navigating the Menu

Settings are saved to the Zen automatically when you exit with B. The menu also auto-saves and closes after 30 seconds of no input.

While the menu is open, inputs are blocked from reaching the game so you won't accidentally shoot or open the Xbox guide.

5. Editing Values (Step by Step)

1 Hold LT + press VIEW to open the menu.

2 Use D-Pad UP/DOWN to highlight the mod you want (e.g. Anti-Recoil).

3 Press A to enter the value editor.

4 Use D-Pad to adjust:

  • LEFT / RIGHT = change by 1
  • UP / DOWN = change by 10

5 If the mod has multiple values (e.g. Anti-Recoil has Vertical and Horizontal), use D-Pad UP/DOWN to switch between them.

6 Press B to go back to the mod list.

7 Press B again to save and exit. The OLED will show SETTINGS SAVED.

7. Toggling Mods ON / OFF

  1. Open the menu (LT + VIEW).
  2. Scroll to the mod with D-Pad UP/DOWN.
  3. Press D-Pad LEFT or RIGHT to toggle.
  4. The Zen OLED shows ON or OFF. The LED blinks green (on) or red (off).
  5. Press B to save and exit.

VM Speed is special: instead of ON/OFF, LEFT/RIGHT cycles through numbers 0–5.

8. Recommended Profiles

Low profile (subtle)

  • Anti-Recoil: ON — Vertical 12–16
  • Polar AA: OFF or ON with Radius 8–10
  • Hipfire AA: OFF
  • Hold Breath: ON
  • Auto Run: ON
  • Slide Cancel: OFF
  • Move Boost: OFF

Balanced (default script settings)

  • Anti-Recoil: ON — Vertical 18, Horizontal 2
  • Polar AA: ON — Radius 12, Speed 18
  • Hipfire AA: ON
  • VM Speed: 3
  • Slide Cancel: OFF

Sniping focus

  • Hold Breath: ON
  • Anti-Recoil: ON — Vertical 10–14 (lower for snipers)
  • Polar AA: ON — Radius 8–12
  • Auto Run: OFF (for positioning)
  • Slide Cancel: OFF

9. Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Menu won't open Hold LT first, then tap VIEW (don't release LT too early). Make sure the correct script slot is selected on the Zen OLED.
RB won't shoot / LB won't ADS Re-compile and re-program the latest script. Confirm in-game layout is Tactical.
Settings don't save Always exit the menu with B — wait for SETTINGS SAVED on the OLED.
Anti-recoil too strong / pulls to floor Lower AR Vertical in the menu or with RS + D-Pad DOWN in-game.
All mods stopped working You may have hit the kill switch. Hold LT + SHARE to turn the script back ON.
Zen not detected on PC Use the PROG port (right side), try a different data USB cable, update firmware in Zen Studio.
